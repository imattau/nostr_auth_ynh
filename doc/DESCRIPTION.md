Sign in to your existing YunoHost account with one or more linked Nostr
identities (NIP-07 browser extension, NIP-46 remote signer, or passkey), as
an addition to password login - not a replacement.

Link a Nostr public key to your account from an already-authenticated
session, then use `/nostr-login` afterwards to sign in with a Nostr signature
instead of a password.
